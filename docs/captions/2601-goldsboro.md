## The one I'd change

Caption 1, the inspection shot. Three things, in order of how much they
matter:

1. **"the opportunity that he has."** On a page the homeowner may share with
   their own friends, this reads as them being sold to. And on a claim job it
   is the same shape as the claim framing that is off the page by hard rule,
   without using any of the banned words. The scan cannot catch this; only a
   reader can.
2. **"where we build trust with the homeowner."** True, and it describes
   Skybird's sales process rather than this roof. It is the one line in nine
   that is about us rather than the work.
3. **"he"** is the only detail about the household anywhere in the set.

Same voice, without those:

> First day on the property, up for the inspection and walking the roof with
> the homeowner. This is where every job starts for us.

Jacob's call. The rest I would not touch.

Smaller: **"blown-off driveway"** is trade vocabulary. "Blown clean" says the
same thing to someone who has never held a backpack blower.
